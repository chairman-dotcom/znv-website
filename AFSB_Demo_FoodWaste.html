What would otherwise be wasted becomes reserve value.

Roughly a third of food produced globally is lost or wasted before it reaches anyone — much of it sold at a steep discount in the hours before landfill, simply because it's imperfect, short-dated, or surplus to one buyer's needs. The AFSB can acquire exactly that stock, at exactly that discount, and preservation turns the same "about to be wasted" cost basis into decades of reserve value. Pick a lot below and run the rescue.
