Module 04

Global risk, watched continuously. Hedges, executed deliberately.

The reserve acquires commodities continuously, but prices move between the decision to acquire and the purchase settling. Hedging isn't speculation — it removes that timing risk so the reserve's cost basis stays predictable. AI watches seven global risk categories and recommends coverage changes as they shift; the desk decides when to act.

Objective

Why the AFSB hedges at all

Lock in an acquisition price before the market moves against it — protecting the reserve's cost basis, not betting on price direction. A hedge that "loses" money when spot falls is still doing its job: it bought certainty.

How AI changes the mechanics

From periodic review to continuous monitoring

Instead of a quarterly risk review, seven global feeds are watched continuously. When conditions shift, the desk sees it and can act within hours — not at the next scheduled meeting.
