About this suite

Apotek is a fictional brand used to prototype an idea, not a real company or product line. The catalogue's product photography, prices, and links belong to their respective real-world brands; the personalization engine is a transparent, rules-based demo running entirely in your browser — no data leaves your device, and nothing here is medical advice.
