Evidence strength — how to read every entry

Strong

Multiple human trials / long clinical track record support the core claim.

Moderate

Some human data or an established mechanism; claims run a bit ahead of it.

Emerging

Plausible and growing evidence base, but still early-stage.

Weak

Marketing outpaces the science; mostly anecdotal or in vitro.

Unproven / Caution

No credible evidence for the claim and/or documented safety concerns.
