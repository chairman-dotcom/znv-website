▸ How the engine reasoned about this

This is a working prototype, not a medical device. The engine above is a transparent, rules-based scoring model running entirely in your browser — it matches your stated goals and constraints against the same evidence-rated dataset behind the Apotek catalogue. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a clinician. In a production version, this same interaction (goals + constraints in, an explainable ritual out) could be powered by an LLM reasoning over real health history, wearable data, and live inventory instead of a fixed scoring table — the UI pattern is the point of this demo, not the specific algorithm.

Apotek automatically keeps Unproven/Caution-rated products (like parasite cleanses) out of auto-generated rituals. Product photography and links belong to their respective brands.
