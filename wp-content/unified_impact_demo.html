      Case study — verifying the road to 100 million mangroves

      How AI ground-truthing would close the loop on the UAE's flagship mangrove target, housed under Erth Zayed's energy, climate & environment sector.

      The challenge

      The UAE's national target is 100 million mangroves planted by 2030 — raised from 30 million at COP26 — aiming for 483 km² of coverage and roughly 115,000 tons of CO&sub2; captured annually. The Abu Dhabi Mangrove Initiative alone has planted more than 44 million since 2020, distributing over 6 million seeds by drone in 2023 alone. At that pace, planting was never the bottleneck — knowing what actually took root is. A drone can seed a tidal flat in an afternoon; confirming survival has always meant a field team wading through mud months later, if it happens at all.

      The AI approach

      This isn't a hypothetical build. Deep-learning canopy classification on Sentinel-2 satellite imagery has already been applied UAE-wide, tracking mangrove extent across Ras Al Khor, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, and Ras Al Khaimah from 2017 to 2024. Separately, AI-guided drone operators already fly UAE restoration sites tracking seed dispersal and seedling health. What doesn't exist yet is a version of this feeding straight into a board dashboard — that's the gap this engagement closes.

      Method: multi-temporal Sentinel-2 canopy classification, cross-checked against IFHC's existing satellite wildlife-tracking network for co-located habitat signal.

      Where to send the field team first

      Ranked by expected information gain — model uncertainty × stakes (zone size) — not raw risk score alone.

      Eastern Mangrove Lagoon outranks Al Zorah here despite a healthier survival rate — it's four times larger, and the model is still genuinely uncertain why its growth curve flattened. A visit there resolves more uncertainty per hour than re-confirming what's already suspected about Al Zorah.

      Why this beats a field survey

      Manual ground survey

      • ~90 days for a full-coverage cycle
      • Tidal-flat access limits when teams can go
      • Cost scales linearly with area surveyed

      Satellite + AI monitoring

      • ~5 days — next clear Sentinel-2 pass
      • No site access required
      • Near-zero marginal cost per additional zone

      Specialized agents, each responsible for one relationship, coordinated by a router. Every outbound message is a draft — an agent never contacts a donor, affiliate, or field team without a human approving it first.
